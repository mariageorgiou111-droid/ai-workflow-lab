Best AI Tools for Solo Businesses: Build a Lean Stack

Independent educational guide. No affiliate links currently installed. This is not a firsthand product review.

The right AI stack is the smallest collection of tools that reliably solves recurring problems. Before subscribing, list your weekly tasks and estimate the time each takes.

1. General-purpose assistants

ChatGPT and Claude can assist with outlines, brainstorming, drafts and organizing ideas. Check accuracy, and do not share sensitive client information without appropriate protections.

2. Transcription and meeting tools

Compare recording consent features, language support, export formats, privacy and retention settings. Always get required permission before recording.

3. Design and content tools

Look for reusable templates, brand consistency, easy editing and useful export options. A general design editor may be sufficient.

4. Workflow automation

Compare integration availability, free-tier limits, error handling and security. Start with low-risk tasks such as transforming your own notes into checklists.

A five-step buying test

  1. Define one recurring problem.
  2. Try a free tier with sample data.
  3. Measure time saved after corrections.
  4. Check limits, pricing and cancellation rules on the vendor site.
  5. Keep the tool only if its benefits justify the cost.
