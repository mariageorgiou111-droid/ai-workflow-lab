Meeting Notes to Action Items: A Safe Automation Workflow

Use AI to structure meeting follow-ups while keeping decisions, names and deadlines accurate.

Workflow

  1. Confirm participants know how notes will be used and obtain any required consent.
  2. Use an approved note source. Remove confidential or identifying details before using an unapproved tool.
  3. Extract only explicit decisions, tasks, named owners and due dates.
  4. Mark missing owners and dates as unconfirmed, never guess.
  5. Have the meeting chair approve the list before updating task systems or emailing anyone.

Reusable prompt

From the anonymised meeting notes below, produce a table of action, owner, due date and supporting note. Include only facts explicitly present. Write “Unconfirmed” for missing fields and flag contradictory statements. Do not invent commitments. [notes]

Quality check

Audit five randomly selected actions against source notes. Track time saved and corrections per meeting. For more detail see the full meeting notes guide.
