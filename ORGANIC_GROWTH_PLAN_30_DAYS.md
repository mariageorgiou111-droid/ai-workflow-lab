# AI Workflow Lab — 30-Day Organic Growth Plan

Prepared: 2026-11-11. Internal editorial plan, not a published website page.

## Objectives
- Build useful, original English-language content for freelancers and solo businesses.
- Confirm Google Search Console sitemap retrieval and indexing before making traffic claims.
- Protect the owner's privacy and avoid paid advertising or fabricated experience.
- Do not add affiliate tracking until the relevant program is approved and disclosures/privacy information are updated.

## Week 1 — Technical verification and measurement
- Check GitHub Pages live URL manually in a browser, including homepage, six guides, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
- In Google Search Console: inspect sitemap status, Pages indexing report, and URL Inspection for homepage and one article.
- Record baseline: indexed pages, search impressions, clicks and queries (use "not available" if none).
- Confirm that the site uses canonical URLs, descriptive titles, meta descriptions, a single clear H1 and internal links.
- Review public GitHub profile, commit author details, and the URL containing the owner's username; do not change the URL without approval.

## Week 2 — Helpful long-tail content
Draft one genuinely useful guide: "How to Write Better Client Emails with AI: 5 Safe Prompts". Use fictional examples, demonstrate before/after editing, disclose that examples are illustrative. Link to Grammarly and ChatGPT vs Claude guides. Publish only after editorial review.

## Week 3 — Second practical guide
Draft "AI Content Checklist for Small Businesses: Fact-Checking Before Publishing" with a reusable checklist and example. Link to Writesonic guide and freelancer workflows. Avoid unverified claims about product features.

## Week 4 — Review and improve
- Recheck Google Search Console and compare indexed pages, impressions and clicks with Week 1.
- Improve any page receiving impressions but few clicks by testing clearer, accurate titles and descriptions; do not promise ranking.
- Audit broken links, accessibility, page rendering on mobile and privacy/disclosure accuracy.
- Decide whether to prepare an additional tutorial based on actual search queries, not assumed keyword volume.

## Editorial checklist for every article
1. Clear search intent and genuinely helpful answer near the beginning.
2. Original examples with no invented hands-on tests, testimonials, statistics or earnings.
3. Descriptive title, meta description, canonical URL, one H1 and meaningful subheadings.
4. Relevant internal links and a link to the provider's official site when discussing a product.
5. Accurate disclosure near recommendations; no affiliate links until approved.
6. No personal name, address, photo or private contact information in page content.
7. Confirm live deployment and indexing separately; publishing does not guarantee indexing.

## Current limitations
- Live website and sitemap could not be independently fetched by the assistant's browsing tools on 2026-11-11; this is not evidence of downtime.
- Search Console data is not accessible through the connected GitHub tools; indexing status is unverified.
- Existing site URL includes the GitHub username. Removing it requires a separately approved URL/account migration.
