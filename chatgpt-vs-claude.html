ChatGPT vs Claude: Which Fits Your Workflow?

This is a task-based comparison method, not a firsthand benchmark or sponsored review. Check current features and prices with each vendor.

Both ChatGPT and Claude are general-purpose AI assistants. The best choice depends on your recurring tasks.

Test 1: Client communication

Use the same fictional client brief and request a concise email. Score clarity, accuracy, tone and time needed to edit.

Test 2: Document summary

Use a public or self-written document and request a summary with evidence. Check every claim against the source.

Test 3: Weekly planning

Give both assistants identical non-sensitive tasks, constraints and deadlines. Compare realism and usefulness.

Choose based on results

Rate each output from 1–5 for accuracy, clarity, format and editing effort. Repeat across several tasks. A free plan may be enough for occasional use; check current subscription details directly.
