How to Automate Customer Email Drafts Safely

Save time on repetitive replies without giving an AI system permission to send messages by itself.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Choose a narrow category such as opening-hours questions or order-status requests.
  2. Keep the original email in your approved inbox; remove names, addresses, order numbers and other personal data from prompts unless a compliant processing arrangement exists.
  3. Use a standard draft template: greeting, confirmed answer, next step, closing.
  4. Have a person check facts, tone, attachments and recipient before sending.
  5. Record time spent and corrections for a two-week pilot.

Safe reusable prompt

Draft a friendly reply to this anonymised customer question: [question]. Use only these verified business facts: [facts]. Do not invent prices, policies or deadlines. If information is missing, ask for clarification. Do not include personal data.

Measure success

Compare average drafting time, error rate and customer satisfaction against your previous process. Include review effort in the AI Savings Calculator.

Approval boundary: AI prepares drafts; an authorized human sends. See data governance safeguards.
