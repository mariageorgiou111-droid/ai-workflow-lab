# Monetization and automation

1. Apply to relevant software affiliate programs only after confirming eligibility and terms.
2. Keep direct vendor links until approval. Clearly label affiliate links on the same page as the recommendation.
3. Do not use deceptive cloaking, cookie stuffing, preloaded retargeting pixels or fabricated reviews.
4. Use Google Search Console to monitor indexing and search performance.
5. Maintain a weekly queue of original posts. Review AI drafts for factual accuracy and platform compliance before scheduling.
6. Review organic traffic, outbound link clicks (with privacy-compliant analytics if added), approved sales and commissions.
7. Expand content only around problems readers actually have. Revenue is not guaranteed.
