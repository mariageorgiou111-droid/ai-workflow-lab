Practical AI Automation for Small Businesses

Start with one repeatable task, measure results and keep a person accountable. Avoid automating decisions you cannot explain or verify.

1. Customer email drafting

Trigger: a non-sensitive inquiry arrives. Workflow: manually remove identifiers → draft a reply using an approved assistant → verify facts, pricing and tone → human sends. Metric: minutes per approved response.

2. Meeting notes into tasks

Trigger: meeting ends. Workflow: use consented notes → extract decisions, owners and dates → flag missing details → manager approves task list. Metric: corrections per meeting and preparation time. Never invent deadlines.

3. Content reuse

Trigger: a published, owned article. Workflow: prepare social drafts → check claims, rights and links → schedule manually. Metric: approved posts per hour, not raw volume.

4. Daily operations briefing

Trigger: start of workday. Workflow: collect approved task/calendar summaries → redact → prioritise → human confirms. Metric: missed actions and minutes saved. Try the sample demo.

5. Cost optimisation pilot

Record baseline time, task frequency, errors, software charges and review effort. Run a two-week pilot. Compare net value in the free calculator. Cancel redundant tools only after confirming no workflow depends on them.

Before you automate

Document an owner, approval gate, failure fallback, access controls and retention rules. Do not paste customer records into unapproved public AI tools. See our data governance guide.
