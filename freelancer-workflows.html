5 AI Workflows for Freelancers

Use AI for first drafts and organization, then review everything before client delivery.

1. Proposal outlines

Provide a fictional brief and ask for objectives, deliverables, scope, assumptions and open questions. Confirm all actual commitments yourself.

2. Notes to action items

With suitable consent and data handling, transform your own meeting notes into tasks, owners and deadlines. Verify every detail.

3. Repurpose your original content

Turn an article you own into a social post, short script and email summary. Edit each for its audience instead of publishing duplicate text.

4. Client onboarding

Generate a checklist for assets, goals, approvals, contacts and timelines, then customize it for your service.

5. Weekly task review

Supply non-sensitive tasks and ask for priorities, dependencies and possible bottlenecks. Make the final judgment yourself.

Keep improving

Track time saved and errors, use reusable templates, and avoid entering confidential information into tools without permission. See our tool selection guide.
